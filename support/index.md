# Support

## Common questions

**What does Earshot remove?** It removes the aircraft and hum tones the published attack uses, replacing them with matching noise, and strips location, date and camera details from the file. The picture is copied untouched.

**What doesn't it remove?** Anything you can see in the picture, such as street signs, landmarks or reflections, stays in the video. Other sounds can still carry clues.

**My clip is too long.** Earshot checks clips up to 3 minutes. Trim it in Photos first: open the video, tap Edit, and drag the ends of the timeline.

**The report found no aircraft tones.** Tones need about 20 seconds to be traced, and very faint ones can sit below the noise. Faint tones can still be matched, so cleaning is still worth it.

**Is my original video changed?** No. Earshot saves a new clean copy to Photos. The original stays as it was; delete it yourself if you no longer need it.

**Earshot can't save to Photos.** Allow Earshot to add photos and videos in Settings › Apps › Earshot › Photos.

**Can I use it from Photos?** Yes. In Photos, tap Share, then Earshot, to check a clip without opening the app. With Earshot Pro you can also clean it right there.

**Why do I see an ad?** Your first clean is free. After that you can clean with a short ad, or get Earshot Pro for unlimited cleans with no ads. You can change your ad privacy choices in the app under About › Ad privacy choices.

**I bought Earshot Pro on another iPhone.** In Earshot, open About and tap Restore purchases. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

## Still stuck?

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Pyxisq/earshot/issues/new). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. Issues are public, so don't attach videos you don't want to share.
