# Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

Earshot is made by an independent developer ("we"). In short: **your videos never leave your iPhone, and Earshot sends nothing about you to us.** People who don't have Earshot Pro may see ads from Google AdMob, described below.

## Your videos

Videos are checked and cleaned on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, to us or to anyone else. Earshot reads only the video you choose, and asks for permission only to add the clean copy to your Photos library.

Earshot keeps temporary working files while it checks or cleans a clip and deletes them afterwards.

## No accounts or analytics

There are no accounts and no sign-up. Earshot contains no analytics. We have no server and receive no data from the app.

## Ads (only without Earshot Pro)

Your first clean is free. If you clean again without Earshot Pro, Google AdMob shows a short ad. Before the first ad, Earshot asks for the consent required where you live (using Google's User Messaging Platform) and, through Apple's prompt, whether apps may track you.

When an ad is shown, Google may collect a device identifier (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can change your choices in the app under About › Ad privacy choices, and tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Earshot Pro users never see ads, and nothing is sent to Google for them.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Earshot only learns whether Earshot Pro is active. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

Earshot is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
